What is this place? 🧪

sadeali.com is a growing collection of small interactive things — games, tools and explorables. Everything runs in your browser: free, no signup, no ads, no tracking.

Who makes it?

SadeAli — I love making things more than talking about myself.

Everything here is open source — you can read the code for every experiment on GitHub.

Why?

The small, handmade corner of the internet is my favorite corner, and I want to add to it. A new experiment appears whenever one is ready — the front page is the catalog, and there's no schedule to disappoint you with.

Enjoying it?

The nicest things you can do are share a page with a friend, or visit the support page. ♥
